# Seismic Risk Mitigation Notes

Seismic risk combines hazard, exposure, and vulnerability. Hazard describes expected ground shaking or fault rupture. Exposure describes people, buildings, infrastructure, and economic assets in affected areas. Vulnerability describes how likely those exposed assets are to be damaged. Risk reduction can include stronger building codes, retrofitting, land-use planning, emergency drills, and public warning systems.

After damaging earthquakes, rapid impact assessment helps emergency managers prioritize rescue and infrastructure inspection. Reliable assessment usually needs both scientific measurements and social information such as population distribution and building inventory.

中文摘要：地震风险通常由危险性、暴露度和脆弱性共同决定。降低风险不仅依靠地震监测，还需要建筑抗震设防、老旧建筑加固、应急演练和公众预警。
